# Research alignment — v0.2

The application operationalizes a personal longitudinal observation workflow around the research protocol's broad framing:

1. Circadian rhythms and biological timing
2. Sleep-wake regulation and recovery
3. Environmental and social synchronization
4. Individual differences and chronotypes
5. Cognitive and behavioral performance
6. Time use, work, and productivity

The interface groups observations into nine practical domains. These are product categories, not validated constructs or a proposed universal taxonomy of human functioning.

The Rhythm Map displays selected self-ratings on a transformed 0–100 axis for visual comparison. It preserves raw values and original units. This is not a psychometric score and should not be interpreted as a clinical or physiological measurement.

A circadian rhythm is not simply any fluctuating line. Claims about circadian periodicity require repeated observations at suitable sampling intervals, control or documentation of masking influences, and appropriate analysis. The current app is an exploratory personal observation tool, not a circadian phase assessment.

Retrospective life-history entries must remain distinguishable from prospective observations. Recall confidence is recorded, but does not convert memory into objective measurement.
